XI JINPING LEADER
DEPENDENCIES & COMPATIBILITY
========================================

Mod Name:
Leader: Xi Jinping

Mod ID:
Limespace_Xi_Jinping-Leader


========================================
OVERVIEW
========================================

This mod adds Xi Jinping as a playable leader in Sid Meier's Civilization VII.

Xi Jinping can be used as a standalone leader and does not require the
China Civilization mod to function.

If the separate China Civilization mod is installed and enabled, additional
compatibility is activated automatically, allowing Xi Jinping to be paired
historically with China in the Modern Age.


========================================
STANDALONE FUNCTIONALITY
========================================

Xi Jinping does NOT require the China Civilization mod.

With only the Xi Jinping Leader mod enabled:

- Xi Jinping is available as a playable leader.
- Xi Jinping's leader ability functions normally.
- Xi Jinping's agenda functions normally.
- Xi Jinping's Memento is available.
- Xi Jinping's narrative events are available.
- Xi Jinping's Advanced Start bonuses are available.
- Xi Jinping may be paired with civilizations available in the player's game.

The China Civilization mod is therefore optional.


========================================
OPTIONAL CHINA CIVILIZATION COMPATIBILITY
========================================

Optional Companion Mod:

Civilization: China

Mod ID:
Limespace_China-Civilization


When both mods are enabled, the Xi Jinping Leader mod detects the China
Civilization mod using Civilization VII's ModInUse criteria system.

Additional compatibility data is then loaded automatically.

This adds:

- China as Xi Jinping's historical civilization.
- A preferred historical pairing between Xi Jinping and China.
- Modern Age China compatibility.
- Historical-choice presentation in the civilization selection interface.

If the China Civilization mod is not installed or is disabled, this
compatibility data is simply not loaded.

Xi Jinping remains playable.


========================================
LEADER ABILITY
========================================

Belt and Road Initiative

Xi Jinping's gameplay is focused on economic development, international
trade, and China's connections with the wider world.

The ability is implemented entirely within the Xi Jinping Leader mod and
does not depend on the China Civilization mod.


========================================
LEADER AGENDA
========================================

National Rejuvenation

Xi Jinping's agenda reflects an emphasis on development, prosperity,
national strength, and long-term growth.


========================================
LEADER ATTRIBUTES
========================================

Xi Jinping uses the following leader attributes:

- Economic
- Political


========================================
MEMENTO
========================================

Red Flag

The Red Flag is Xi Jinping's custom Memento.

Effect:

+10% Gold in Cities.

The Memento, its artwork, localization, unlock data, and gameplay effect
are included with the Xi Jinping Leader mod.


========================================
NARRATIVE EVENTS
========================================

Xi Jinping includes custom narrative events across all three Ages.


ANTIQUITY

1100 - Mandate of Heaven
1101 - Prosperity of the Realm
1102 - Defend the Realm


EXPLORATION

1200 - Strength of the Nation
1201 - Routes Across the World
1202 - National Rejuvenation


MODERN

1300 - Reform and Development
1301 - China on the World Stage
1302 - The Chinese Dream


These events use Xi Jinping-specific localization and gameplay rewards.


========================================
ADVANCED START
========================================

Xi Jinping includes custom Advanced Start choices for later Ages.

EXPLORATION

- International Cooperation Initiative
- Maritime Security Initiative


MODERN

- Global Development Initiative
- Air Power Initiative


These choices provide different diplomatic and military strategic options
when beginning the game in a later Age.


========================================
DEPENDENCY SUMMARY
========================================

REQUIRED:

- Sid Meier's Civilization VII


OPTIONAL:

- Civilization: China
  Mod ID: Limespace_China-Civilization


The China Civilization mod is NOT a hard dependency.


========================================
COMPATIBILITY BEHAVIOR
========================================

Xi Jinping only:
    Xi Jinping loads and functions as a standalone leader.

China only:
    China loads and functions as a standalone civilization.

Xi Jinping + China:
    Xi Jinping gains his historical/preferred Modern Age China pairing.


========================================
INSTALLATION
========================================

Install the Xi Jinping Leader mod in the Civilization VII Mods directory
and enable it from the game's Additional Content menu.

The China Civilization mod may also be installed and enabled if the
historical Xi Jinping / China pairing is desired.


========================================
MOD STRUCTURE
========================================

The Xi Jinping Leader mod contains its own:

- Leader configuration
- Leader gameplay data
- Leader ability
- Leader agenda
- Leader artwork
- Leader colors
- Localization
- Diplomacy dialogue
- Memento
- Narrative events
- Advanced Start content
- Historical China compatibility configuration


========================================
IMPORTANT
========================================

Do not rename the internal leader identifier:

LEADER_Xi_Jinping

The mod uses this identifier throughout its configuration, gameplay,
localization, narrative, Memento, and UI data.

The China compatibility layer expects:

CIVILIZATION_CHINA

and the companion civilization mod ID:

Limespace_China-Civilization
